# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI data visualization and analytics project** designed to analyze coffee shop sales performance.

The dashboard provides insights into **revenue, profit margin, quantity sold, unit price, product categories, branches, shops, years, and quarters**. Interactive filters allow users to explore the sales data from different perspectives and identify important business trends.

The main objective of this project is to transform raw coffee shop sales data into meaningful and easy-to-understand visual insights that can support better business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall coffee shop sales performance.
* Track total revenue and profit margin.
* Understand product category performance.
* Compare sales across different branches and cities.
* Analyze quantity sold for different product categories.
* Compare unit prices across different shops.
* Analyze yearly profit margin trends.
* Understand quarterly revenue and profit performance.
* Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Revenue by Product Category

A column chart is used to compare revenue generated by different product categories such as:

* Desserts
* Sandwiches
* Tea Beverages
* Coffee Beverages
* Specialty Drinks
* Snacks
* Cold Drinks

This helps identify which product categories contribute more to overall revenue.

### 2. Revenue by Branch

A donut chart shows the distribution of revenue across different branches/cities.

This allows businesses to compare branch performance and identify high-performing locations.

### 3. Profit Margin by Year

A yearly comparison is provided for:

* 2021
* 2022
* 2023

This helps understand how the profit margin changes over time.

### 4. Quantity Sold by Product Category

A pie chart represents the quantity sold across different product categories.

This helps identify customer purchasing patterns and popular product categories.

### 5. Unit Price by Shop Name

An area/line chart compares the total unit price across different coffee shops.

This provides a clear view of price variation between shops.

### 6. Unit Price by Branch and Product ID

A horizontal bar chart compares unit prices across branches and product IDs.

This helps analyze product-level pricing differences between locations.

### 7. Profit Margin Gauge

A gauge visualization displays the overall profit margin against the selected scale.

The current dashboard shows a total profit margin of approximately **16.23K**.

### 8. Revenue and Profit by Quarter

A quarterly visualization is included to analyze changes in revenue and profit across:

* Q1
* Q2
* Q3
* Q4
